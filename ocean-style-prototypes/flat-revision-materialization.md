# Flat silhouette revision: verified output provenance

Five approved compositions were generated by Codex's native `image_gen.imagegen`. Their exact native staging filenames and depth mapping are retained in `flat-revision-source-map.json`.

The CLI incorrectly treated staged image outputs as unsaved in-memory data and attempted oversized base64/interactive writes. The parent recovered the actual native PNG files from Codex staging, visually verified the tier identities, and copied their bytes unchanged. Approved copies are isolated in `creature-approved-sources/minimal-silhouettes/`; alternative CLI retries are not the inputs to the final exported images.

The five final minimal-silhouette overlays were exported from those approved native files, preserving real alpha and fitting the drawings onto 1600 × 900 canvases. The other 25 creature exports and all ocean backgrounds were unchanged. Previous minimal exports and evidence were retained rather than deleted.

After the outputs were captured, exported, and browser-tested, the dedicated revision CLI process tree was stopped to end its unnecessary write-retry loop. No further generation is needed. This cleanup does not claim that its wrapper produced a successful final report; the actual files, native source map, current manifest, and captured browser response establish the result.
